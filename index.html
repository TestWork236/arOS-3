Express settings

We recommend these settings, which include occasionally sending
info to AbdiRPC. You can customize these settings now or later.

  • Cloudbay is your default cloud storage.
  • Automatically connect to Wi-Fi networks.
  • Use Webwide as your default browser.
  • Turn on 'Do Not Track' in Webwide.
  • Help improve AbdiRPC software, services, and location
    services by sending us diagnostic information.
  • Check online for solutions to problems.
  • Let apps give you personalized content based on your PC's
    location, name, and other data.
  • Turn on sharing and connect to devices on this network.
