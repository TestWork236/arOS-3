Activate arOS

You need to enter a valid product key. Your product key should be in the
confirmation email you received after purchasing arOS, or inside the packaging
that arOS came in.

If you select "Skip", arOS will run on a 30-day trial period, after
which it will become unusable until you enter a product key to activate it.
